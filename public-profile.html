About Joshua

I am a seasoned Proposal Manager and Bid Specialist with over 8 years of hands-on experience in Nigeria's oil & gas industry. My expertise spans the full bid lifecycle — from invitation to tender (ITT) analysis and pre-qualification documentation to technical write-ups, commercial submissions, and post-bid negotiations.

I have worked with and delivered winning proposals for major IOCs and EPCs including Seplat Energy, TotalEnergies Nigeria, Julius Berger, and NNPC Ltd. My proposals have collectively secured contracts in excess of ₦2.8 billion. I bring deep knowledge of HSE documentation, HAZOP processes, regulatory compliance (DPR/NUPRC), and cost estimation frameworks relevant to the Nigerian project economy.
