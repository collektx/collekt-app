🔒 Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 8, 2026 • Effective Immediately

Welcome to Collekt (operated by Collekt Technologies Ltd, accessible at https://collektng.xyz). Collekt connects Nigerian engineering specialists, EPC contractors, and bid specialists with oil & gas operators and enterprises.

Your privacy and the security of your personal data are paramount to us. This Privacy Policy details the types of personal and organizational information we collect, how it is used and secured, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, secure, and improve our project marketplace services:

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Credentials: Full name, email address, secure password hash, role selection (Specialist / Company).
  • Professional Profile Data: Technical discipline, years of experience, professional bio, portfolio documents, curriculum vitae (CV), and hourly or project rates.
  • Corporate Identity & Compliance Data: Company name, Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) RC/BN registration number, Federal Inland Revenue Service (FIRS) Tax Identification Number (TIN), official corporate address, and representative contact information.
  • Identity Verification Data: National Identity Number (NIN) and government-issued identification cards for verification badge issuance.

B. Information Collected via Google OAuth (Sign in with Google)

When you choose to register or sign in to Collekt using Sign in with Google, Google provides us with specific information from your Google account upon your explicit authorization:

  • Google Profile Information: Your primary Google account name, email address, unique Google account identifier, and profile picture avatar.
  • Scope of Access: We strictly request read-only access to standard authentication profile scopes (openid, https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email, and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.profile). We do not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail, Contacts, or any other Google workspace services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize the collected information for the following legitimate purposes:

  • Authentication & Security: To verify your identity, process account sign-ups, authenticate logins, and protect against unauthorized access or fraud.
  • Marketplace Matching: To enable enterprises to discover certified Nigerian engineering talent and to allow professionals to submit proposals and bids on active projects.
  • Transactional Communication: To deliver critical operational notifications, including account activation receipts, bid submission status, contract award alerts, and escrow wallet updates.
  • Compliance & Verification: To verify company registration and ensure all users on the platform adhere to Nigerian legal and regulatory standards.

🛡️ Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
Collekt's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We treat your personal data with utmost confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or Google account data to third parties or advertisers under any circumstance.

We only share information with vetted infrastructure service providers necessary to operate the platform:

  • Database & Authentication: Supabase (PostgreSQL with Row-Level Security and encryption at rest and in transit).
  • Transactional Email: Resend (for delivering account security codes and platform notifications).
  • Payment Processing: Paystack (for secure Nigerian Naira card processing and virtual escrow accounts).

4. Data Storage, Security & Retention

  • All data transmissions are protected using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / SSL) encryption.
  • Database records are secured behind strict Row-Level Security (RLS) policies, ensuring users only have access to their own data.
  • We retain your account data for as long as your account is active on Collekt. You may request account deletion at any time.

5. Your Rights and Data Deletion

You retain complete control over your personal data:

  • Access and Rectification: You can review and edit your profile details at any time via your user dashboard.
  • Revoke Google Access: You can disconnect Collekt from your Google Account at any time through your Google Account Security Settings.
  • Account Deletion: You may request permanent deletion of your account and all associated profile and transaction records by emailing us at collektng@gmail.com. Upon receipt, your personal data will be completely purged from our active databases within 48 hours.
