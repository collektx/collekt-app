01 The Logo
Collekt
'">
COLL → People & Talent
EKT → Projects & Industry

The Collekt wordmark encodes the platform's dual purpose directly into its letterforms → inspired by the VAIO logo's philosophy of letters with dual meaning. The organic flowing COLL represents people, talent and human connection. The angular, precise EKT represents projects, engineering and industry. Together they tell the whole story in one word.
